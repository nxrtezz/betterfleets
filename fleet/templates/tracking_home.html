Vehicle Tracking

Choose a tracking mode to begin tracking your vehicle journey.

Unscheduled Tracking

Select a vehicle, choose stops on a map, and create a custom journey. Best for ad-hoc or special services.

Scheduled Trip

Select an operator, service, and trip from the timetable. Ideal for regular scheduled services.

Tracking Only

Simple vehicle tracking with route number and destination. Minimal setup, just track your position.

How it works

  1. Choose a tracking mode above
  2. Set up your journey details (vehicle, route, stops)
  3. Start tracking - your location will be sent to the server
  4. View your progress on the map with live updates
  5. Stop tracking when you complete your journey
