Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 May 2026

Better Fleets is operated by EeveeIT Ltd. Most pages can be used without creating an account or sharing personal details.

If you create an account or submit edits, we store the information needed to run those features safely. This can include your email address, profile details you choose to publish, and your IP address where needed to help prevent abuse or vandalism.

If you contact us, we use the details you send so we can reply. We may pass your message on to a bus operator, transport authority, or another relevant organisation if that is the best way to help, unless you ask us not to.

If you would like a copy of your account data, want your profile removed, or need help with a privacy request, please contact us.

Cookies and local storage are used for core site functions such as remembering that you are logged in and preserving map preferences.
