# 🚀 BetterFleets is Live!

Hey everyone! We're excited to announce that BetterFleets is now live and ready to use. Here's what you can do:

## 📝 Vehicle Logging
Log all the vehicles you've ridden and track your fleet completion stats. See how many vehicles you've ridden for each operator and your overall completion percentage.

**Link:** https://betterfleets.org/fleet/

## 🔧 Vehicle Edits
Submit edits to correct vehicle information like registration, fleet number, operator, livery, and more. Edits go through an approval process to keep data accurate.

**Link:** https://betterfleets.org/vehicles/edits/

## ⭐ Vehicle Reviews
Rate and review vehicles you've ridden. Share your experiences with the community and help others know what to expect.

**Link:** https://betterfleets.org/ (browse vehicles and leave reviews)

## 🤖 Discord Bot
Our Discord bot makes it easy to log vehicles right from Discord. Use these commands:

- `/log <vehicle>` - Log a vehicle as ridden
- `/check <vehicle>` - Check if you've logged a vehicle
- `/unlog <vehicle>` - Remove a vehicle from your log
- `/completion <operator>` - View your completion stats for an operator
- `/link <code>` - Link your Discord account to your BetterFleets account

The bot now shows nice embeds with vehicle details including registration, fleet number, operator, livery, and logged status!

## 🎮 Driver Logging
If you're a driver, you can log vehicles you've driven (separate from ride logs). Request driver status from your profile to get started.

**Link:** https://betterfleets.org/accounts/request-driver-status/

## 🏆 Scoring & Leaderboard
Earn points for contributing to the site:
- +10 points for approved edits
- +5 points for published reviews
- -5 points for disapproved edits
- -2 points for hidden reviews

Check out the leaderboard to see top contributors!

**Link:** https://betterfleets.org/accounts/leaderboard/

## 🔗 Account Linking
Link your Discord account to your BetterFleets account for easy access to the bot and features.

**Link:** https://betterfleets.org/accounts/discord-link/

## 📊 Public Profiles
View your profile with your stats, reviews, and contributions. You can also make your fleet completion stats public for others to see.

**Link:** https://betterfleets.org/accounts/users/
